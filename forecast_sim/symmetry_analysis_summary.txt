BOT/HUMAN SYMMETRY -- 100-RUN STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
======================================================================
100 full-scale runs (200 players, 40 events, spec economics: $300k/$1.5M/$3M dividend tiers, 0.25% transaction fee, $10,000 flat auction fee, 0.02%/event cash yield, no new-entrant growth).
Grid: 5 population-wide strategy-mix presets x 4 bot:human ratios x 5 seeds.

Comparison is ORIGINAL-cohort humans (n=50, present since t=0) vs. bots
(n=100-500 depending on ratio) -- same tenure, same strategy-draw
distribution. Under the fix (bots draw strategies identically to humans),
the expected gap is 0 in the long run; any nonzero mean here should be
explainable as sampling noise (bots are a MUCH larger sample than the 50
original humans, so bot averages are less noisy -- that alone does not
imply bias, just that the human side of the comparison is noisier).

1) OVERALL (all 100 runs pooled)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   mean ELO gap (orig human - bot): -3.14  (std 20.29, SE 2.03)
   one-sample t-test vs 0: t=-1.55, p=0.122  -> NOT significantly different from 0 (consistent with no systematic bias)
   mean ROI gap (orig human - bot): -0.16pp  t=-1.86, p=0.063  -> not significant
